FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything international patients and NRIs commonly ask before choosing orthopedic care in India.

General & Clinical Network
Is MedTrip India a hospital or a travel agency?
Neither. MedTrip India is a doctor-founded healthcare coordination platform. We help international patients understand their orthopedic treatment options, compare accredited hospitals in India, and receive structured support throughout the process — without bias toward any single hospital.
Do you only work with one hospital in India?
No. MedTrip India works with a vetted network of JCI and NABH-accredited hospitals across India. We do not restrict patients to a single facility. Our coordination team helps identify which partner hospitals are best suited for your specific procedure, budget, and travel situation.
How do you select your partner hospitals?
We use strict medical and operational benchmarks. Our partner facilities must hold national (NABH) or international (JCI) quality accreditations, maintain ultra-low infection rates, utilise advanced surgical technologies (like robotic systems), and feature dedicated international patient wings with bilingual staff.
What is the benefit of a "Holistic" approach to surgery?
Traditional hospitals often focus solely on the surgical operation. Through our network partners, we emphasise a dual-track approach: modern allopathic surgery combined with post-operative holistic rehabilitation and nutrition protocols to speed up recovery and mobility.
Medical & Technology
What kind of technology is used for joint replacements in your network?
Our network centres use Active Robotic-assisted surgical systems. Robotic surgery ensures sub-millimetre precision tailored to your anatomy, resulting in less tissue damage, minimal blood loss, and faster return to walking compared to conventional surgery.
What brands of medical implants are used?
Our partner institutions utilise exclusively internationally recognised, FDA-approved medical implants from world-leading manufacturers (such as Zimmer Biomet, Stryker, or Smith & Nephew). The exact implant model is selected by the operating senior orthopaedic consultant based on your age, bone density, and lifestyle requirements.
Logistics & Travel Support
How do I get a medical opinion and price quote before travelling?
The process is entirely remote and free. Upload your recent X-rays, MRI reports, or case summary via our WhatsApp link or website form. Our coordination team shares your file with partner orthopedic specialists, then responds within 48 hours with suitable hospital options and an indicative cost estimate.
Can I bring a family member or attendant with me?
Yes, absolutely. We highly recommend travelling with an attendant for emotional and physical support. All our partner hospital packages naturally include accommodation and complimentary meals for one accompanying caregiver right inside your private patient room.
How do local airport pickups and transport work?
Once your flight arrival is confirmed, a dedicated hospital vehicle with a patient liaison will be waiting at the airport to drive you directly to the hospital or your pre-booked hotel — no need to navigate a new city on your own.
Visas & Legalities
How do I arrange my Medical Visa for India? Can I travel on a tourist visa?
International patients must travel on a valid Medical Visa (MED) or Medical Attendant Visa (MED-X) — not a tourist visa. Once you approve your plan, the partner hospital's international desk issues an official Medical Invitation Letter, which you use to apply for an Indian e-Medical Visa online (typically processed in 2–5 days).
What happens if I need to extend my stay in India due to medical recovery?
If your treating consultant recommends staying longer for rehabilitation, our team alongside the hospital's international desk handles the legal paperwork. We facilitate the official medical certificate and file for a local visa extension with the Foreign Regional Registration Office (FRRO) so you can focus entirely on recovering without visa stress.
Costs & Financial Transparency
Are there hidden fees or surprise charges upon hospital discharge?
No. One of the core missions of MedTrip India is complete financial transparency. We work with our partner hospitals to provide comprehensive, fixed-package pricing upfront. Your written quote will explicitly state exactly what is included (surgical fees, theatre charges, routine medications, implants, and room stay) and what is excluded — completely eliminating "bill shock."
How do I pay for my treatment? Do you accept international insurance?
Payments are made directly to the hospital's billing desk upon admission. Hospitals accept major international credit/debit cards, wire transfers (SWIFT), and USD or local currency cash. For international insurance, we can provide itemised discharge summaries, certificates, and billing receipts to help you file for reimbursement with your provider back home.
Why is healthcare so much more affordable in India compared to the West?
The lower cost is purely due to lower operating overheads, local pharmaceutical manufacturing, and lower cost of living in India — not a compromise on quality. The robotic equipment, FDA-approved implants, and clinical safety protocols used by our network surgeons are identical to, and often surpass, those found in Western institutions.
