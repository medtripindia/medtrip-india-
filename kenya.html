Questions

Common questions

Can family travel with me?
Yes. Their stay is usually included in your package.
Is the hospital accredited?
Yes. SGVP is NABH-accredited — India's top hospital safety standard.
What if my case is complex?
We explain expected costs and requirements before you travel.
What is included in the estimate?
The estimate covers the expected surgery, implant, hospital stay, and early recovery costs. Your final quote follows X-ray review.
Do I need to bring my X-rays?
Send clear photos or scans on WhatsApp first. Bring the originals when you travel if the hospital asks for them.
Can I speak to a doctor before travelling?
Yes. We can arrange the case review and explain the hospital's response before you decide to travel.
